02 · Schema

Postgres Data Model

Five schemas, one Postgres database (Neon, serverless). core and knowledge are the corpus; search is the vector/FTS index; learner is student state; pipeline is operational metadata. Click a schema tab, then a table, to drill into the legacy corpus columns. The P0 pedagogical extension is documented separately below; applying the migration and auditing live population are distinct from defining the schema.

P0Pedagogical system-of-record contract

Learning objectives and relations

Skill objectives carry optional levels 1–5, stable identifiers, unique slugs, names and measurable objectives. Skill-to-Concept membership, Skill-to-Skill composition, and prior-to-dependent prerequisites are separate facts. Projection must preserve their identity and provenance; a hierarchy alone cannot establish a prerequisite.

Problem associations

REQUIRES, PRACTICES, and TESTS keep their distinct meanings and retain role, required_level, importance, confidence, source, and review_status. Level is an objective requirement, not measured learner mastery.

Multidimensional difficulty

conceptual_depth, technical_load, algebraic_load, insight_required (nullable 1–5), number_of_steps, prerequisite_depth, and estimated_contest_level retain provenance and approval status. Preserve unknowns; do not coerce missing dimensions to zero or treat the existing difficulty_band as complete pedagogical evidence.

Review is an explicit gate

Generated records remain PENDING until separately reviewed. Stored provenance, populated rows, graph projection success, and human approval are different checks. Generated confidence is never approval. The derived graph inventories PENDING, REVIEWED and REJECTED metadata, while tutor evidence filters remain reviewed-only.

Relationship directions and query contract · Teaching evidence semantics · Detailed P0/P1/P2 requirements. P1 steps, stored hints, misconceptions and courses, and P2 skill-level learner personalization remain planned.

The hybrid retrieval index: chunked text + embeddings, model-versioned.

search.representation → search.chunk → search.embedding

A pipeline, not a single table: core.problem.statement_text becomes a representation (PROBLEM_STATEMENT or SOLUTION_FULL), which is split into chunks (max 6,000 chars), each of which gets an embedding per active model. search.chunk.textsearch is a generated tsvector column — this is what full-text search runs against for the lexical half of hybrid retrieval.
